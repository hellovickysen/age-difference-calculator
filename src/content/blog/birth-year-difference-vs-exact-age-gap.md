Subtracting two birth years gives only a rough answer. It does not check the month or day.

For the correct answer, use both full dates in the [Age Difference Calculator](/#calculator).

## Different years, but only one day apart

Person A was born on 31 December 2000. Person B was born on 1 January 2001.

If you subtract the years, the answer looks like 1 year. But the real gap is only **1 day**.

## Same year, but many months apart

Person A was born on 1 January 2000. Person B was born on 31 December 2000.

The birth-year difference is zero. But the real age gap is almost one full year.

These examples show why the year number alone is not enough.

## How to find the exact gap

Use this simple method:

1. Enter the older person’s full date of birth.
2. Enter the younger person’s full date of birth.
3. Count full years, then full months, then the days left.

The calculator does these steps for you.

## When birth years are useful

Birth years can be useful for a quick general idea. For example, they can show that two people belong to different age groups.

But do not use birth years alone when you need the exact gap. Always use the full dates for forms, records or any careful comparison.

## Simple rule

Use birth years for a rough answer. Use full dates of birth for the exact answer.
