To find the age gap between two partners, compare their full dates of birth.

Enter both dates in the [Age Difference Calculator](/#calculator). The earlier date belongs to the older partner.

## Find the exact gap

The calculator counts full years, then full months, then the days left.

For example, if the result is 3 years, 4 months and 10 days, that is the fixed age gap. It will not change as the partners grow older.

## Exact answer and simple answer

In daily conversation, people may say “about three years”. This is a rounded answer.

For records or a clear comparison, use the complete answer: 3 years, 4 months and 10 days.

Both ways can be useful. Choose the one that fits the situation.

## What the number means

The result tells you only the time between two birth dates. It does not tell you anything about the quality of a relationship.

## Be careful with “ideal age gap” claims

There is no calculator that can decide the perfect age gap for every couple. Relationships depend on many personal, family and legal matters.

If a person is below the legal age for marriage, check the current official law. Do not depend on a general blog or calculator for legal advice.

## Protect personal information

You do not need to share full birth dates publicly. Use the calculator privately and share only the final gap when possible.
