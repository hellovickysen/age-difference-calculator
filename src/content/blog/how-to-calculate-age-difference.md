You need two full dates of birth to find the correct age difference. Do not compare only the birth years. That can give the wrong idea.

[Use the Age Difference Calculator](/#calculator) to get the answer quickly. Enter both dates and select **Calculate age difference**. You can enter the dates in any order.

## Easy steps

Start with the earlier date. This belongs to the older person. Then count:

1. Full years
2. Full months after those years
3. Days left after those months

For example, compare 4 June 1988 and 19 January 1993.

- Four full years take us to 4 June 1992.
- Seven full months take us to 4 January 1993.
- Fifteen days take us to 19 January 1993.

So, the age difference is **4 years, 7 months and 15 days**.

## Why the full date matters

Imagine one person was born on 31 December 2000. Another person was born on 1 January 2001. Their birth years are different, but they are only one day apart.

This is why you should use the day, month and year for both people.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Do not subtract only the birth years.
- Do not treat every month as 30 days.
- Check whether the date is in DD/MM/YYYY format.
- Use a four-digit year, such as 1995 instead of 95.
- Check the dates once before trusting the answer.

In India, dates are commonly written as DD/MM/YYYY. For example, 04/06/1988 means 4 June 1988.

## Does the age gap change?

No. The exact gap between two birth dates always stays the same. The whole-number ages people say in daily life may look different between birthdays, but the real date-to-date gap does not change.
