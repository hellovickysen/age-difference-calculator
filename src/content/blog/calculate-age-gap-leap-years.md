A leap year has 366 days instead of 365. The extra day is 29 February. This day can change the total number of days between two birth dates.

The [Age Difference Calculator](/#calculator) checks leap years automatically.

## Simple leap-year rule

A year is usually a leap year when it can be divided by 4. But years ending in 00 have another rule. They must also be divided by 400.

Examples:

- 2024 was a leap year.
- 2028 will be a leap year.
- 1900 was not a leap year.
- 2000 was a leap year.

You do not need to remember this rule when using the calculator.

## Years, months and days

The calendar answer counts full years, full months and the days left. A leap day may be inside that period, but it does not always add one to the day part shown at the end.

## Total days

The total-day answer includes every day between the two dates. If 29 February falls inside the period, it is counted.

This is why the same number of calendar years can have a different number of total days.

## If someone was born on 29 February

The date is valid only in a leap year. When comparing it with a date in a normal year, the calculator follows a clear month-end rule.

Official rules may treat a leap-day birthday differently for age limits or legal work. Always follow the rule written on the official form or notice.

## Quick test

Try dates that cross February in 2024. Then compare similar dates in 2023. The total-day answer may differ by one day because 2024 includes 29 February.
